THE 12 VEHICLES (left-facing line-art side profiles):

1. IS-2 — Heavy tank, long hull with sharply sloped front glacis at roughly 60°. Six large overlapping road wheels with small gaps between pairs. Large rounded, low-profile turret sits well back on hull, overhanging the rear. Extremely long, heavy 122mm gun barrel projects far forward — the longest gun of any tank here. Signature: massive gun barrel length relative to hull, rounded low turret.

2. ISU-152 — Fully turretless assault gun on a KV-series hull. A large, boxy, heavily sloped casemate superstructure fills the entire front two-thirds of the hull — no turret at all. Six large road wheels visible. A huge-diameter, stubby but wide 152mm howitzer-gun protrudes from a central ball mount in the casemate front plate. Signature: enormous casemate almost as tall as it is long, and the widest gun muzzle of any vehicle here.

3. KV-1 — Heavy tank with a thick, flat-sided rectangular hull and near-vertical glacis (less slope than T-34). Six large road wheels with small gaps. The turret is a boxy, roughly octagonal multi-faceted shape — wider and more angular than the IS-2 turret, sitting roughly centered on the hull. Medium-length 76mm gun barrel. Signature: boxy multi-faceted turret on a thick, upright-fronted heavy hull.

4. KV-2 — Same long KV hull and six large road wheels as the KV-1, but topped by an absurdly TALL, massive rectangular box turret — nearly as tall as the hull itself, with flat vertical sides and a flat top, making the vehicle's total height roughly double a normal tank. Short, large-calibre 152mm howitzer projects from the turret face. Signature: the enormous slab-sided box turret dominates — unmistakable silhouette twice the height of any other turret here.

5. T-34/76 — Medium tank with a distinctively sloped hull: steep glacis at ~60° and sloped sides. Five large road wheels with clearly visible gaps between each. The turret is small and rounded, almost hexagonal in section, sitting toward the rear of the hull. Short-to-medium 76mm gun barrel. Signature: compact rounded turret and steep hull slopes on a relatively small, low vehicle.

6. T-34/85 — Same T-34 hull and five large road wheels as the /76 but with a noticeably larger, wider, more rounded turret with a larger-diameter turret ring. The 85mm gun barrel is longer than the /76's and extends further forward. Signature: bigger, rounder turret clearly wider than the /76, and a longer gun barrel — main visual differentiator from its sibling.

7. T-70 — Small, very low-profile light tank. Four small-to-medium road wheels with gaps. The hull is lightly sloped at the front. A small oval/rounded turret offset to the left of centerline, low and flat. Short 45mm gun barrel. Signature: distinctly small and squat compared to all other tanks here, tiny turret, only four road wheels visible.

8. SU-85 — Turretless tank destroyer on a T-34/76 hull, five large road wheels. A low, sloped casemate superstructure replaces the turret — the casemate is trapezoidal in side profile, wider at the base, fully enclosed and armored. A long 85mm gun barrel protrudes centrally from the casemate front. Signature: fully enclosed low sloped casemate (no turret), long gun on a T-34 running gear.

9. SU-76M — Small turretless self-propelled gun. Four road wheels on a lighter chassis (shorter and narrower than T-34). A low, boxy, open-topped casemate sits at the rear of the hull — the top edge is open (no roof), with thin side walls visible. Medium-length 76mm gun projects forward from the casemate front. Signature: open-top rear casemate with no roof, lightweight small hull clearly smaller than the SU-85.

10. M3A1 Scout — Open-top four-wheeled armored scout car with rubber tyres. Low, boxy armored hull with a nearly vertical front plate. Two large front wheels and two rear wheels clearly visible. Open fighting compartment at the rear with low walls. No main gun — light machine-gun ring mount on the rear compartment. Signature: wheeled (not tracked), open-top box body, visibly smaller and rounder than any tank.

11. Lend-Lease Halftrack (M5) — American-built M5 halftrack in Soviet service. Front end has two large rubber-tyred wheels on a conventional axle. Rear half of the vehicle rides on a short tracked bogie with small road wheels and a visible drive sprocket — the hybrid front-wheel / rear-track silhouette is unique. Open-top personnel box body with low armored sides. Signature: front wheels + rear track — the only vehicle here with this mixed running gear.

12. US6 Studebaker — American Studebaker US6 6×6 cargo truck in Soviet service. Long, high hood over the front engine compartment followed by a flat open cargo bed. Three axles visible: one front steering axle with large rubber tyres and two close-spaced rear drive axles beneath the cargo bed — making six wheels total visible from the side. Cab sits high and boxy. Signature: three-axle multi-wheel truck profile with high engine hood and long flat cargo bed — the only purely wheeled multi-axle heavy truck here.

Final reminder: identical thin white line-art stroke on every icon, flat pure-black background, NO text anywhere.
